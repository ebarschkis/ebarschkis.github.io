About

Hi, I’m Enrique.

I work on open problems in computer science and mathematics, mostly in number theory. I’ve solved two previously open Erdős problems and am working on a few other conjectures.

I also spend time exploring elliptic-curve cryptography and integer factorization. More recently, I’ve worked on photonic chips for running LLM inference. Much of what I’m working on ends up here.
